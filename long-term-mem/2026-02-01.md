## Key Learnings

### Scope Clarification
Current approach does **pattern matching**, not **root cause analysis**:
- ✅ "This session matches known anomaly patterns"
- ✅ "This differs from normal sessions in these ways"
- ❌ "The failure was caused by X" (requires domain model)

This is the correct scope for traceable explanations - we match patterns and cite evidence, we don't diagnose.

### Signature Cards Purpose
Provide **domain knowledge context** to the LLM:
- Session `BGL_00442780` matches "Machine Check Exception"
- LLM now knows this indicates "serious hardware error detected by CPU"
- Enables richer explanations beyond "looks similar"
